## Biggest changes in one paragraph

The round-2 revisions close four blocker-class contradictions and harden the money and authorization cores. "All branches" is now a nullable `branch_id` plus an `all_branches` flag with partial unique indexes instead of a sentinel UUID that could never satisfy the composite foreign keys. Refunds are bound to a single ledger representation: positive `amount_minor` rows of `payment_type='refund'`, with the requirements' "negative payment" phrasing explicitly superseded. Phase numbering is canonical (plan Phases 0–17) everywhere, and the ADR-41 fallback gate now points at a phase that exists. Refunds/voids are owner/manager-only with receptionists forbidden, `platform_admin` is gone from the role enum in favor of audited impersonation, blocked-time writes go through the cross-entity booking lock, and every tenant-owned foreign key is composite with attack-path tests. The unsafe round-1 SQL drafts are quarantined under `sql/drafts-v1/` with banners, and a CI clean-migration gate proves the rewritten schema, generated types, functions, and adversarial fixtures all build together. Six new ADRs (47–52) cover rate limiting, data residency, backups/PITR, offboarding, the deterministic checkout calculation order, and the round-2 model additions (branch calendar preferences, client source). The MVP also gained the missing home/today screen (US-DASH-1), a taxes summary report, and the import software epic Phase 8 depends on.
